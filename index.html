About Us

A safe, non-judgemental space for your mind.

CGPRC is a mental wellness clinic offering professional psychological care for children, teens, adults and families. Every plan is personalised and every session is private.

Our approach combines Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, mindfulness and supportive counselling, so you build skills that last beyond the session room.

FAQ

Common questions answered.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Everything you share is private and handled with professional ethics.

How does booking work?

Fill the form, pay by UPI, and enter the transaction number. Once the admin approves, your booking is confirmed.

How long does approval take?

Usually within a few hours during clinic hours. Check status in "Track Your Booking".

Can I take sessions online?

Yes, choose "Online Session" and the link will be shared on WhatsApp after approval.

What if my booking is rejected?

It usually means the payment could not be verified. Contact the clinic with your payment screenshot.
